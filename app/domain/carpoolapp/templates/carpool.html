Everybody who needs a ride is put in the car of the earliest driver with a seat free, in the order they signed up, within a minute of their sign-up counting — once it is paid for, or approved, where the form asks for either. A rider is texted when they are put in a car. A driver is texted when they first offer to drive, and after that a summary of who is in their car once it has been quiet for ten minutes. Every text links to the person's own ride page. This form's organisers are emailed when somebody is left without a car, as they are about its submissions. {{if not .Texting}}Texts are off on this service, so nobody is being texted: call or message them yourself.{{end}}
