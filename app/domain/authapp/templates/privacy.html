Privacy

This service runs the forms of the Schoenstatt Fathers and the people they serve: registrations, sign-ups and orders. This page says what it keeps and why.

Answers to a form

What you type into a form is kept so that the people running it can read it: the office, and whoever they have given access to that form. If you pay, the payment is made on Stripe's own page; card numbers never reach this service. Your address is used to send you a receipt and, where the form allows it, a link to change your answers. Your IP address is kept with a payment for Stripe's fraud checks and is not shown to anyone.

Accounts

People who run forms sign in with a code sent to their email address. The service keeps that address, their name, and which forms they may read.

Google Sheets

Somebody who may read a form's answers can connect a Google Sheet, so that a spreadsheet of those answers in their own Google Drive is kept up to date. When they do:

  • Google asks them to allow this service to create files in their Drive. It can reach only the spreadsheet it makes for them, and nothing else in their Drive, their email or their account.
  • It learns which Google account they connected, to show them.
  • It keeps the permission Google gives, encrypted, and uses it only to update that spreadsheet with the answers they could already read here.
  • Nobody else here is told that the spreadsheet exists.
  • It stops when they disconnect it, when their access to the form ends, or when they remove the permission in their Google account (myaccount.google.com, under Security → third-party connections). Disconnecting withdraws the permission at Google; the spreadsheet stays theirs.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Nothing received from Google is sold, shared, or used for anything but keeping that spreadsheet.

Questions

Reply to any email this service has sent you, or write to the office of the form you filled in.
